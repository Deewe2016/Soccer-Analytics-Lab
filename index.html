How the model works

The model combines Elo with attacking and defensive indicators, chance creation, shots, possession, and recent form. It converts the resulting expected goals into scoreline and win/draw/loss probabilities.

This is an educational model. Its weights should be backtested against historical matches before treating it as a calibrated forecasting system.
